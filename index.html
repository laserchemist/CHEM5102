🔍 Data Analysis and Evidence CHEM 5102 / CHEM 3102 — Forensic Chemistry Data Labs

Forensic chemistry involves the forensic application of analytical chemistry theories, techniques and instrumentation to the analysis of controlled substances, fire debris evidence, explosives, and other trace evidence/chemical unknown materials. This course covers concepts relevant to the field of forensic chemistry including chain of custody, and the theory and practical application of measurement uncertainty and error propagation, alongside the classification and unique physical and chemical characteristics of forensic evidence.
